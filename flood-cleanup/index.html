Whether the water came from a storm, an overflowing river, a backed-up sewer, a failed sump pump or a burst plumbing line, flood damage needs a trained crew right away. Floodwater is almost always rated Category 2 (gray water) or Category 3 (black water), so it carries bacteria, contaminants and possibly hazardous material that can make people seriously ill. For flood emergencies anywhere in Fayetteville, Fayetteville Restoration Co is available 24/7 to extract the water, disinfect affected areas and bring the property back safely.

We start with fast water extraction, using truck-mounted and submersible pumps that can move thousands of gallons per hour. With the standing water gone, our IICRC-certified technicians check the contamination level and apply the right sanitizing steps, including antimicrobial treatment, HEPA air filtration and disinfection of every affected surface. We tear out and properly dispose of what cannot be saved, such as soaked carpet padding, contaminated drywall and ruined insulation.

After extraction and sanitizing, we set up commercial drying equipment, including LGR (low-grain refrigerant) dehumidifiers and high-velocity air movers, to dry the structure of your property thoroughly. Daily readings from calibrated moisture meters and a thermal imaging camera tell us when everything is fully dry, which is what keeps mold from growing. Every stage of the job is recorded for your claim: photographs, detailed moisture maps, logs of the equipment used and the scope-of-work reports that insurance adjusters require.

Why Professional Flood Cleanup Matters

Flood cleanup is far more than pumping out standing water. Floodwater carries bacteria, sewage, chemicals and other contaminants that are serious health hazards. Professional flood crews are trained in containment, personal protective equipment (PPE) and sanitizing protocols that keep your family from exposure to pathogens such as E. coli, hepatitis and other waterborne diseases.

Beyond the health concerns, a poor flood cleanup often leaves hidden moisture that turns into severe mold growth within days. Our technicians rely on psychrometric calculations, meter readings and thermal imaging to verify that each affected spot, from the framing to the subfloor to the inside of the walls, meets accepted drying standards. Drying by measurement is the one dependable method for avoiding secondary damage, which can add thousands to the repair bill.

Maintenance Tips for Flood Cleanup

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Add downspout extensions so roof water empties well away from the foundation
  • Coat basement walls with waterproofing and seal any cracks in the foundation
  • A battery-powered backup sump pump keeps water moving if the electricity fails in a storm
  • Debris in a gutter or storm drain blocks proper flow, so clean them out regularly
  • Have a backwater valve fitted to the sewer line so sewage cannot flow back inside
  • Regrade low spots so soil around the whole foundation falls away from the walls

Signs You Need Flood Cleanup in Fayetteville

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • Dirty water rising out of floor drains or sewer lines into rooms you use
  • An overflowing washing machine, dishwasher or water heater
  • Soft, wet drywall or carpeting that is soaked through
  • Seepage at door thresholds, window wells or cracks in the foundation
  • A water line on walls or baseboards showing how high it rose
  • Receding water that leaves muddy residue and debris behind
  • A sump pump that failed during heavy rain and let the basement flood
