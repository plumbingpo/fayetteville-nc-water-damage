A sewage backup is one of the most hazardous situations a property owner can face. Raw sewage contains dangerous bacteria, viruses, parasites and other pathogens, including E. coli, hepatitis, salmonella and giardia, that pose serious health risks to anyone who touches contaminated materials. Fayetteville Restoration Co provides emergency sewage cleanup throughout Fayetteville, responding 24/7 with properly trained technicians, appropriate personal protective equipment and EPA-registered disinfectants to decontaminate your property safely.

Sewage damage is classified as Category 3 (black water), the most contaminated and dangerous category of water damage. That rating calls for the strictest protocols: containment barriers go up, crews wear full protective equipment, every porous material the sewage touched is removed, and the surfaces that stay are disinfected thoroughly. For Category 3 water damage the reference is IICRC S500, and our crews follow it to bring the building back to safe occupancy.

We clear the standing sewage first, then pull contaminated water out of structural materials, remove carpet, padding, insulation, drywall and any other porous item that was in contact with sewage for proper disposal, disinfect each hard surface, treat with antimicrobial products and finish with structural drying, which prevents secondary mold. To finish, thermal foggers and hydroxyl generators of industrial grade deodorize the space so the sewage smell does not linger.

Sewage Cleanup Prevention Tips

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • Find your sewer cleanout now and check its condition, so it can be reached in an emergency
  • Keep cooking oil, grease and food scraps out of the drain, where they build into clogs
  • Use drain strainers in showers and sinks to catch hair and debris
  • Have roots removed where trees grow by the sewer, before they get inside the pipe and plug it
  • A drain that is slowing down should be dealt with early, while it is still partly open
  • Route sump pump and gutter water away from the sewer line, which can back up when storm flow overloads it

Signs It Is Time for Sewage Cleanup

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • Strong sewage or rotten egg odors coming from drains or lower levels
  • Dark, dirty water or sewage rising out of a tub, toilet or floor drain
  • Multiple drains in your home backing up at the same time
  • Every sink, tub and shower draining slowly even after you plunge them
  • People in the household getting stomach or intestinal symptoms with no clear cause
  • A cleanout that weeps sewage, or wet foul patches in the lawn along the sewer path
  • Damp patches on a wall or floor that also smell foul

Why Hire a Professional for Sewage Cleanup?

This is one cleanup you should never take on yourself. Handling raw sewage safely takes specialized training, EPA-registered disinfectants that consumers cannot buy, and personal protective equipment, meaning respirators, Tyvek suits and protective boots and gloves. Hepatitis, gastrointestinal disease and infections of the skin or respiratory system are among the serious illnesses these pathogens cause.

Contaminated materials count as biohazard waste, and disposing of them properly is part of a professional cleanup. Putting carpet, insulation or drywall soaked with sewage out with household trash breaks the law and endangers sanitation workers and your community. Licensed biohazard disposal facilities take this waste from certified restoration firms, which keep the required protocols in place.
